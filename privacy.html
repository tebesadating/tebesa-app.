Tebesa Dating - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Tebesa Dating ("Tebesa", "we") is a dating app for adults. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using Tebesa you agree to this policy.

1. Who can use Tebesa

Tebesa is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the app. If you believe a person under 18 has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

2. Information we collect

  • Telegram account details when you log in: your Telegram ID, first name and username (if you have one).
  • Profile information you give us: name, date of birth, gender, who you want to meet, city or region, religion, education, bio, interests and your photos.
  • Activity in the app: profiles you like or pass on, matches, blocks and reports, and the messages you send in chats.
  • Payment proof: if you buy Tebesa Gold, the Telebirr receipt image or receipt details you send us so we can confirm your payment.
  • Basic technical data such as your IP address, used to protect the service from abuse.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account and show your profile to other adult users.
  • To suggest people and create matches based on your profile and preferences.
  • To let you and your matches send messages to each other.
  • To confirm payments and give you Gold features.
  • To keep Tebesa safe: handling reports, blocking abuse and preventing fraud.
  • To send you notifications about matches and messages.

4. What other people can see

Your profile (name, age, photos, bio, interests, city or region and the other details you choose to fill in) is visible to other users of Tebesa. Your messages are visible only to the person you are chatting with. Do not share information you do not want others to see.

5. Who we share information with

  • Telegram, which provides your login and delivers some notifications.
  • Our hosting provider (Supabase), which stores the data and runs our servers for us.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Keeping your information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, matches and messages, except where we must keep limited records for legal or safety reasons.

7. Your choices

  • You can edit your profile and photos inside the app.
  • You can block and report other users.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by contacting us at the address below. Please send the request from the email or Telegram account linked to your Tebesa profile so we can confirm it is you.

8. Security

The app communicates with our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make important changes we will tell you in the app.
